# Equipment artwork

`brush-mark.svg` is an original geometric P/G stencil drawn for this project.
It uses paths and solid colors, with no font, downloaded logo or photograph.
It supplies the dark molded housing color and off-white printed equipment marks
on the existing brush mesh. The marks sit at the two ends, clear of the socket.
The rounded mesh's planar UVs keep them on the top face. Mipmaps filter the print
in game views. The small SVG remains ordinary source text, not a binary LFS asset.

Manufacturer photographs under ignored `build/reference/` were consulted for
proportions and the appearance of printed plastic equipment. They are not shipped.
